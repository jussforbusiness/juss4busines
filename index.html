We collect only the minimum information necessary:

  • TikTok Access Token — Required to authenticate with TikTok's API. Stored locally on your device only.

Your information is used solely for:

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data. Your access token and video files remain on your local machine.

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. Delete your data anytime by removing the Application's data files.
